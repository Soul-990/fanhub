Narrative Overview

Following the great synthetic collapse of 2084, humanity segregated into heavily fortified corporate megacities governed by quantum mainframe syndicates. In the neon shadows of Neo-Kyoto, a disavowed cyber-operative known only as Ronin Cipher discovers a sealed artifact dating back to feudal Japan — the Chrono Blade. Imbued with tachyonic crystal resonators, the weapon does not merely cleave steel; it allows its wielder to pause, rewind, and rewrite three-second intervals during sword strikes.

When the omnipotent Zaibatsu Consortium deploys hunter-killer android squads to seize the relic, Cipher joins forces with spirit alchemist Aiko Kitsune. Together, they embark on a perilous pilgrimage across subterranean bullet-train tunnels and abandoned mountain temples to unlock the origin of the blade before timeline entropy collapses the universe.
